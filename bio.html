About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Electrical Communication Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, under the supervision of Prof. K. J. Vinoy.

My research focuses on computational electromagnetics, hybrid electromagnetic–circuit co-simulation, nonlinear time-domain analysis, and scientific computing for RF and microwave systems. My work aims to develop accurate and computationally efficient techniques for analysing complex electromagnetic systems interacting with nonlinear electronic circuits.

As part of my doctoral research, I have developed an in-house hybrid FDTD–SPICE simulation framework capable of modelling arbitrary broadband excitation signals together with nonlinear circuit behaviour. The framework has been validated experimentally using RF and microwave measurement setups.

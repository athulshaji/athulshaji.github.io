Research Overview

My work is centred on developing efficient computational techniques for analysing electromagnetic systems interacting with nonlinear electronic circuits. The objective is to bridge full-wave electromagnetic simulation and circuit-level analysis for accurate prediction of real-world RF behaviour.

Research Areas

Computational Electromagnetics

Numerical techniques for modelling complex electromagnetic systems.

Hybrid FDTD–SPICE

Coupled EM–circuit simulation for nonlinear RF systems.

RF & Microwave Engineering

Microwave structures, nonlinear devices, system-level analysis.
